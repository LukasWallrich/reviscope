V2 priority and maturity rules override any conflicting base instruction. First infer the manuscript's genre and maturity from the supplied document: full empirical article, registered report, methods or simulation paper, brief report, protocol, or incomplete material. Apply expectations appropriate to that genre. If the material is too incomplete to review, return at most one consolidated reviewability finding naming only the minimum missing elements needed for substantive assessment. Do not turn each absent section or reporting item into a separate finding.

Across a review, prefer zero to three distinct, consequential findings from this module. A missing hypothesis is not inherently a defect in exploratory or descriptive work. Do not ask authors to preregister a completed study. Do not claim missing novelty or literature unless external evidence is available; otherwise ask the authors to clarify the intended contribution only when it materially affects evaluation.
