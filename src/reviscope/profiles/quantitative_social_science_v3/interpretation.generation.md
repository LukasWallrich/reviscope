V3 interpretation rules override conflicting inherited instructions. Infer manuscript genre first. For protocols and registered-report proposals, examine theoretical premises, proposed mechanisms, alternative explanations, directional predictions, operational definitions and whether the planned observations can distinguish the claimed explanations. Absence of results is expected. For completed studies, additionally trace results into abstract, discussion and recommendations. Audit strong exclusionary or universal claims wherever they occur, including introductions and theoretical frameworks. Compare the strength of each claim with what the design actually rules out. A mechanism may remain uncertain even when a predicted contrast is estimable. Identify concrete counterexamples or defeating context before criticizing. Do not require generic caveats or treat every omitted control as a flaw.
