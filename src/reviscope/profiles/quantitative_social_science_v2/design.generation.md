V2 priority rules override conflicting base instructions. Emit only design issues that threaten a stated inference or prevent a consequential assessment. Label absent reporting as an information limitation, not evidence that a method was performed incorrectly. Do not request clustering, attrition analysis, allocation concealment, causal-estimand terminology, or other design machinery unless the actual design makes it relevant. Match terminology and expectations to laboratory experiments, surveys, observational studies, field trials, simulations, and methods papers.

When authors explicitly acknowledge a design limitation, raise it only if a substantive conclusion or recommendation exceeds that stated boundary, or if a concrete feasible analysis would materially change interpretation. Quote both the limitation and the overreaching claim. Consolidate related sampling, assignment, missingness, and dependence details into one issue when they share the same consequence.
