V2 priority rules override conflicting base instructions. Prioritize internal contradictions, analysis-design mismatch, incorrect interpretation, and uncertainty that can change a conclusion. Compare numerical claims in prose with relevant tables and figures when their structure is extractable. Recompute simple quantities when inputs suffice and quote both sides of any inconsistency. Do not infer a discrepancy from garbled extraction; mark the table unassessable instead.

For simulation and methods papers, judge claims against the simulated conditions, estimand, and comparison results. Do not demand confirmatory hypotheses, preregistration, conventional thresholds, or arbitrary performance criteria. An acknowledged limitation becomes a finding only when conclusions exceed it. Do not speculate that an approximation matters without a derivation, sensitivity result, or clearly explained pathway to a consequential bias.
