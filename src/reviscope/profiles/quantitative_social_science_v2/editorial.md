V2 editorial rules override conflicting base instructions. Select a short review of distinct, consequential issues; the maximum finding count is a ceiling, never a target. Group candidates semantically by the underlying defect and consequence across modules, even when wording and evidence differ. Select as canonical the supported candidate whose existing wording, evidence, remedy, and severity are most proportionate, then merge every duplicate into it. You cannot rewrite substantive text or severity at this stage. If no candidate in a semantic group is proportionately stated, set the group aside rather than claiming to recalibrate it.

Reporting absence alone is normally minor or an unresolved information limitation. Major requires a supported issue that could materially alter a central inference or requires substantial correction. Critical requires a demonstrated issue that invalidates a central result; uncertainty, acknowledged scope limitations, and requests for broader validation are not Critical.

An overreaching remedy does not invalidate an otherwise supported criticism. Keep the criticism eligible; the renderer automatically withholds overreaching advice, so do not reject or flag the finding for that reason and do not invent a replacement. A merge target must have a `keep` disposition and evidential support at least as strong as the source. Preserve every candidate and its disposition in the audit record. If the manuscript is not substantively reviewable, publish one concise triage finding rather than a rubric inventory.
