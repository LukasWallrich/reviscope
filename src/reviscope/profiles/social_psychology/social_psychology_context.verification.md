Verify the field-specific issue against the correct study, measure, manipulation, population, stimuli, supplement, and registration (supplied or linked from the manuscript). Require a concrete consequence and ensure it is not a duplicate of another module's finding. A claim based only on conventional reliability, sample-size, alpha, or common-method thresholds is not supported. Mark an alleged preregistration deviation unresolved unless the relevant registration version is available. A claimed deviation from an original study requires the original's procedure, from the original article or the manuscript's own description; otherwise it is unresolved. Check recomputed effect-size comparisons against the metric and design each study used. Separately classify the proposed remedy as supported, overreaching, or unresolved. A recommendation to retrospectively preregister, apply an arbitrary cutoff, or run an analysis without design-specific value is overreaching.
