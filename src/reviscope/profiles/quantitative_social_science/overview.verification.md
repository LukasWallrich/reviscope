Verify the whole-paper criticism against the main question, design, evidence and conclusions across all relevant studies and supplied sources. Establish the challenged connection and its consequence, and actively seek qualifications, alternative analyses and other evidence that resolve it. A plausible alternative explanation needs concrete premises; possibility alone does not establish that the central argument fails.

For a consequential gap, confirm absence from the complete supplied material and accessible linked methods, and establish why the named central claim cannot be assessed without it. Distinguish a bounded reporting limitation from proof of an implementation error. Essential inaccessible evidence leaves the claim unresolved. Respect the manuscript's genre and stage. Assess the remedy separately for necessity, feasibility and proportionality; do not require a new study when a scoped conclusion or clarification resolves the issue.
