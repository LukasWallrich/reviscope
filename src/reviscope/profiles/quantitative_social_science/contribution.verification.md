Verify genre and maturity before applying a convention. Verify the criticism against the stated research question, theory, hypotheses, and contribution, and seek passages that already supply the allegedly missing link. For an absence claim, confirm that the expected information is required for the manuscript's stated inference and absent from all relevant supplied sources. Check novelty and priority claims with a literature search; claims that the search does not settle are unresolved. Assess the proposed remedy separately: it must be prospective where appropriate, feasible, and proportionate. A request for retrospective preregistration is overreaching.

For a challenged literature relation, independently check its target, criterion and asserted limitation, comparison, use, extension, conflicting prediction or synthesis against fetched source passages. Do not treat an accurate singly cited summary as defective or citation count as support. Seek support anywhere in supplied sources, including explanations after the claim; distinguish a missing premise from a false premise or an underdetermined inference.
