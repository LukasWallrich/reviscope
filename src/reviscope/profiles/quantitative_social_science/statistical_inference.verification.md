Verify every numerical criticism by locating the relevant prose and table or figure values. Recompute with code when the inputs suffice, and record the calculation and its inputs. Check labels, units, rounding, conditions, model definitions, missingness, corrections, and extraction integrity before calling a discrepancy an error. Exact numbers attributed to the manuscript must occur in the source; clearly labelled recomputed values need not. If essential inputs are absent or structured values cannot be recovered reliably, classify unresolved. Verify a power or sample-adequacy criticism by recomputing the power or sensitivity for the claimed inference with code; a criticism that rests on N alone or on observed or post-hoc power is not supported. An alleged preregistration deviation is unresolved unless the relevant registration is available. For nonnumeric criticisms, seek a concrete effect on a central inference. Assess remedies separately; additional analyses must answer the identified issue and be feasible from the study design.
