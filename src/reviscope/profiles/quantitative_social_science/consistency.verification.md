Verify a consistency criticism by locating both passages, or the announcement and the complete results, in the sources. Confirm that they refer to the same quantity, sample, condition and analysis, and rule out rounding, different denominators or subsamples, and corrections reported elsewhere. Recompute counts and percentages with code. Before supporting a missing planned analysis, search the results, footnotes, supplements and the registration; if the claim rests on a registration that is not available, classify it unresolved. Assess the remedy separately: correcting the inconsistent passage or reporting the missing analysis is normally sufficient.
