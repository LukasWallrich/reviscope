Verify a coherence criticism by locating the specific broken link: the claim, the premises it relies on, the inferential step and the conclusion that depends on it. Search all supplied sources, including later sections, footnotes and supplements, for a premise or qualification that repairs the link; support may follow the claim or appear in another section. Classify the criticism as supported only when the link fails as described and the stated conclusion depends on it. For an alleged contradiction, confirm that the two passages make substantive claims about the same thing that cannot both hold under any reasonable reading. For a rival-prediction criticism, confirm that the rival account plausibly predicts the same result; whether a rival theory exists in the literature requires a source. Keep disagreement about importance separate from factual support. Separately classify the remedy as supported, overreaching, or unresolved; prefer a scoped wording or framing change when it fully resolves the problem.
