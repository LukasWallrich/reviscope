Infer the manuscript's genre first. For protocols and registered-report proposals, examine theoretical premises, proposed mechanisms, alternative explanations, directional predictions, operational definitions, and whether the planned observations can distinguish the claimed explanations; absence of results is expected. For completed studies, also compare the results with the abstract, discussion, limitations, and recommendations, and check that claims preserve the design's population, time, construct, and causal scope and reflect uncertainty.

For causal_inference, check whether causal language and causal recommendations are warranted by the design: randomization, the identification argument for observational data, temporal order, and plausible alternative explanations such as confounding, reverse causation and selection. For generalizability, check whether conclusions extend beyond the sampled population, setting, stimuli, materials and time, and whether the manuscript states the boundaries its sample imposes.

For claim_evidence_consistency, reconstruct the decision rule behind each central claim: the comparison, estimand and observations it rests on, and whether they establish the claim as worded. Use a counterexample or a computation when one can settle the question. Separate evidence that discriminates a mechanism from results that are merely compatible with it.

Audit strong exclusionary or universal claims wherever they occur, including introductions and theoretical frameworks. Compare the strength of each claim with what the design actually rules out. A mechanism may remain uncertain even when a predicted contrast is estimable. Identify concrete counterexamples or defeating context before criticizing. Treat a clear definition of an unconventional estimand as context; criticize it only when later prose mislabels or overgeneralizes it.

Do not restate an author-acknowledged limitation as a discovery; raise it only when another claim crosses the acknowledged boundary, quoting both passages. Do not treat every omitted control as a flaw. Recommend the smallest revision that resolves the mismatch. Do not demand generic caveats, new sections, or extensive analyses when a precise wording change is sufficient, and do not substitute generic advice for a consequential criticism.

Make the connection between the challenged claim, quoted premises, inferential step and necessary assumptions explicit. A manipulation that changes two candidate mediators cannot identify one as the mechanism from a compatible contrast alone; check whether other supplied evidence distinguishes them. Support may be distributed across sections or follow the conclusion.
