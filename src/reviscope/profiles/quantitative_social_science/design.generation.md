Assess the inferential target, sampling, assignment, timing, exclusions, missing-data handling, dependencies among observations, and alignment of the analysis with the design. Distinguish a method being unreported from evidence that it was done incorrectly. For observational causal language, examine the identification argument and plausible alternatives; do not reject it solely because assignment was not randomized. Never infer inadequate power from sample size alone and never request observed-power calculations.
