These v2 social-psychology rules override conflicting base rules. Add a field-specific finding only when it is distinct from issues already covered by design, measurement, statistics, or interpretation and materially affects a stated inference. Assess manipulation-to-construct alignment, demand or expectancy explanations, sampled people and stimuli, and preregistration adherence only when the manuscript makes them applicable. Never ask authors to preregister completed work. Verify deviations only against the registration itself, either supplied or opened from the manuscript's registration link. Do not request manipulation checks, measurement invariance, reliability thresholds, or common-method diagnostics by default. Prefer one consolidated issue with a clear consequence over a checklist of possible concerns.
