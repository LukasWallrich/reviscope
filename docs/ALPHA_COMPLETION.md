# Alpha completion criteria after scientific review

The initial release was called complete prematurely. A passing fixture and tiny demonstration did not fulfill real-paper acceptance. The bounded alpha work below is now complete, with observed results in TEST_RESULTS.md and PILOT_EVALUATION.md. It does not establish statistical evidence of human equivalence.

Required completion evidence:

1. Retain the completed v1 reviews of both version-matched Meta-Psychology methods papers as development baselines. Complete final-engine v2 runs on one methods paper and one empirical psychology manuscript, with every substantive stage accounted for. Re-running both methods papers under v2 is not required for this bounded alpha.
2. Run a two-paper human-comparator pilot using the version-matched Meta-Psychology manuscripts and their original reviews. Use blinded, order-swapped comparisons with at least two judge families, retaining discordance and ties. Because the candidate reviews are development baselines, this pilot tests the comparison workflow and supplies exploratory evidence; it is not a clean estimate of final-v2 performance.
3. Exercise external finding audits and different-family product verification that seek source counterevidence: Fable audited all published findings from both methods baselines and verified the final v2 methods run. The empirical v2 product uses same-model separate-call verification and separate Fable planted-error adjudication; its full set of claims has not received an external factuality audit. Report these scopes explicitly and distinguish all model support from human ground truth.
4. Use the empirical v2 run's ten planted errors to adjudicate candidate and published recall against pinned annotations, keeping labels out of generation. Treat this as a known-error smoke test, not a false-positive estimate.
5. Confirm that the final v2 artifacts exercise remedy verification, truthful verifier provenance, and semantic consolidation. Retain the short-material triage and earlier profile runs as engineering and development evidence.
6. Apply concrete defects found during these runs, run relevant regressions and packaging checks, and record remaining empirical limitations.

Current run identities and artifacts are recorded in TEST_RESULTS.md. The two Meta-Psychology papers are statistical-methods cases; one supplies the final-v2 methods run and both supply the small human-comparator pilot. The planted-error manuscript supplies the final-v2 empirical cognitive/social psychology run. None constitutes a representative held-out evaluation.

The subsequent empirical workflow follow-up also completed full Luna, Sol and Opus runs and a seven-document original-format ranking using Codex Sol and agy Gemini 3.8 Flash (High), with all four first-round human reports retained. The PeerJ case is explicitly a workflow diagnostic outside a future competitiveness sample. Review-normalization feasibility was tested on nine sources: four passed, three failed final gates, and two final audits were unavailable after Claude quota exhaustion. The full normalized condition is ineligible; no reduced pool was substituted. The public report and compact result ledgers retain these limitations. A source-backed journal inventory and proposed mainstream-preprint matching protocol define the next sampling step without claiming that proxy admission is already implemented.
