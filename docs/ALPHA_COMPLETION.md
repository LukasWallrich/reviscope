# Alpha completion criteria after scientific review

The initial release was called complete prematurely. A passing fixture and tiny demonstration did not fulfill real-paper acceptance. This record tracks the remaining bounded alpha work; it does not promise statistical evidence of human equivalence from a small pilot.

Required completion evidence:

1. Complete current-engine Luna/max reviews of both version-matched Meta-Psychology papers, with all substantive stages accounted for.
2. Run actual blinded, order-swapped comparisons against their original human reviews, with at least two judge families; retain discordance and ties.
3. Independently assess the published criticisms with Fable, including source counterevidence. Report model-assessed support separately from human ground truth.
4. Run a blind empirical psychology manuscript with ten planted errors; adjudicate candidate and published recall against the pinned annotations, keeping labels out of generation.
5. Exercise the revised scientific profiles and remedy verification, input-insufficiency handling, truthful verifier provenance, and semantic consolidation. Preserve earlier profiles and results as development baselines.
6. Apply concrete defects found during these runs, run relevant regressions and packaging checks, and record remaining empirical limitations.

Current run identities and artifacts are recorded in TEST_RESULTS.md when completed. The initial two Meta-Psychology papers are statistical-methods cases; the planted-error manuscript supplies an empirical cognitive/social psychology case. None constitutes a representative held-out evaluation.
