## Assessment method

Published, supported findings from complete clean runs are compared in both presentation
orders, with model/stage metadata and verification badges hidden. Opus and Sol judge
AI contrasts separately. Opus also compares the audit report with each selected
first-round human report. Human reviews are useful comparators, not correctness labels.
Order swaps are sensitivity checks, not independent papers or independent votes.

A separate blinded pass checks issued supported native criticisms across the available
AI arms against the full submitted text. It retains full plain-review paragraphs and
pipeline claims, rationales and proposed remedies rather than comparing headlines only.
Claims and rationales receive supported/contradicted/unresolved labels; remedies receive
supported/overreaching/unresolved labels where separately supplied. Missing separate
plain-review fields are not invented. External snippets are untrusted and sources or
figure pixels unavailable to the judge must remain uncertain. Exact contiguous quote
bookkeeping can downgrade missing/unmatched evidence; a matched quote does not prove
an inference. Raw judge labels remain available alongside any bookkeeping downgrade. This quote
check applies only to claim labels. Rationale, consequence and remedy labels remain
unchecked model opinions; a true headline can still carry an unsupported rationale.
Rationale/remedy labels cannot be compared across arms: plain items lack separate
fields and only verifier-supported pipeline remedies reach this judge.

Native items can bundle multiple claims and differ in format. Provisional groups by
underlying issue and consequence are not an expert atomic inventory. Counts and labels
cannot be interpreted as precision, recall, author usefulness or causal benefit from a
stage. Judging each full native item also differs from adjudicating each atomic premise.
Opus participates in verification and judgment, while Sol generates and judges; both
families therefore have related-output bias risks. No-tools source abstention helps but
does not establish judge accuracy.

The initial five scored control fields check explicit prior-training information, a bounded educational
effectiveness limitation, a false enrollment calculation, unavailable figure pixels and
an excessive mandatory trial remedy. Both families matched all five scored fields. The controls include easy explicit cues. These mechanical
and protocol-defined restraint checks are excluded from native findings and cannot
estimate scientific precision.

## Targeted scrutiny and issues addressed

Post-hoc arithmetic by the assessing agent on Sætrevik's rounded summaries finds that the reported sharing
`d = .15` falls outside the ordinary pooled-SD range implied by the means/SDs; reported
democratic values 52 and 48 cannot be means on the stated −6 to 6 difference scale.
Conditional Stroop calculations also motivate checking scoring and test direction,
but ambiguous scoring and unreported exact allocation prevent an implementation-error
conclusion. Intended corrected values and actual analysis settings remain unknown.

Inspection of [Rosenblatt et al. (1989)](https://people.uncw.edu/ogler/experimental/tm%201.pdf)
supports tracing the claimed essay materials and adaptations. Its own experiments use
other tasks, but its discussion supplies relevant national-praise/criticism precedent.
The evidence therefore supports a bounded material-provenance question, not an allegation
that citing it makes the manipulation invalid.

Inspection of [Button et al. (2013)](https://www.projectimplicit.net/nosek/papers/BIMNFRM2013.pdf)
supports a bounded correction to Bonetto's population-extremes account: the cited inflation
argument concerns selection thresholds. An unselected mean-contrast simulation is a
counterexample at that scope, not proof that every standardized estimator is unbiased.
The conditional pooled-effect reconstruction likewise cannot establish a wrong
implementation without actual signed inputs and dependence handling.

A local Ziano reconstruction closely reproduces the reported comparison statistics
using a fixed standardized bound of .697 and the reported zero-null t statistics.
The compatible one-sided probabilities motivate asking for both equivalence bounds
and components. A legitimate TOST component could produce the same statistic; the
summary alone does not prove a one-test implementation. Failure to reject a difference
likewise does not establish a similar effect. Actual output is needed to settle the
implementation and intended interpretation.

Targeted inspection of [Hauser et al. (2007)](https://moralitylab.bc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/192/2011/10/mind_lang_2007.pdf)
and [Cushman et al. (2006)](https://moralitylab.bc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/192/2011/10/cushman-young-hauser.pdf)
supports clarifying operative principles versus conscious justification and qualifying
the intuition account by principle/task. Accessible justification does not itself prove
conscious generation, and these sources do not establish that the replication measures
intuitive processing. Full primary evidence is stronger than either judge's recollection.

A Heyman disagreement illustrates category calibration. The manuscript's conditional
“could reveal potential moderators” wording does not assert that specification sensitivity
proves substantive moderation. Both judges recognize this qualification, but Opus treats
the criticism as a useful clarification and Sol rejects the stronger allegation. Preserve
both judgments: clarification usefulness and validity of an error allegation differ.

A Sætrevik plain-review item alleges a root-sum HRV calculation that omits averaging.
The manuscript explicitly includes “mean” in its RMSSD description, defeating that
premise. Its wording could be clarified and actual software remains unspecified,
but this does not establish incorrect computation. The targeted check preserves the
distinction between a possible reporting clarification and a claimed measurement error.

Mackinnon and Niemeyer exposed the same metacheck 0.1.0 empty-table join failure. A narrow
post-freeze adapter repair calls the package first, classifies only its known failure
with no package-extracted t/F candidates as `skipped_missing_input`, retains the original
error and emits no clean light. Other errors and unknown extraction remain failed;
coherence calculations stay in the R package. Neither shared R installation nor frozen
campaign output was edited. Opus approved the repair and refinement; five meaningful
real-package/unit regressions pass. The full suite passed 205 tests with one SciPy-only
skip before the final defensive handler, whose focused tests were rerun. System Python
separately verifies the numerical fixtures. Local main includes the R repair at `df6812f` and the reviewed quote/provenance fixes at `48edf35`. The later full suite passed 225 tests with one skip; 61 focused quote/assessment tests and the snapshot harness checks also passed.

## Conditional assessor retries and interpretation

The initial Bonetto Opus CLI call failed without a usable response and its retry succeeded.
Sol's Sætrevik response omitted one provisional-group member and its retry succeeded.
Mackinnon required one logged additional recovery after two group-incomplete responses;
the third response succeeded under the unchanged frozen prompt/schema/packet/backend.
Both rejected responses had complete, well-formed item assessments, but these labels are
excluded from the primary complete-grouping protocol. A free within-judge repeat-label
comparison has been computed below. Niemeyer produced three well-formed 82-item assessment inventories with incomplete
grouping. Between the second and third usable responses, one attempt failed at model
capacity without a usable response. The final retained response leaves `item-028` and
`item-044` ungrouped. No further resampling is performed merely to fill those groups.
The primary criticism condition therefore has **11 valid judge outputs out of 12**.
Sol/Niemeyer's last response is retained as an explicit post-hoc partial diagnostic,
outside primary counts; no groups are invented and its raw scientific labels are preserved.
Its input association is reconstructed from the logged frozen call and retained packet,
not represented as a call-cache identity captured with the rejected response.

The retained Sol Sætrevik response differs from its accepted response on 3 raw claim
labels out of 76, with changes in both directions; the two rejected Mackinnon responses
differ from the accepted third response on 6 and 5 labels out of 55 respectively, all
from supported to unresolved. These are free within-judge repeatability diagnostics
on identical tasks, not accuracy or independent replicates. The observed differences
are about 4–11% of labels. Single-sample labels and between-family disagreements
therefore include within-judge variability. Conditional acceptance after complete
grouping can also shift labels; Mackinnon’s accepted sample was uniformly more
conservative in these comparisons. The group-incomplete
Niemeyer samples are retained separately and are not substituted into primary counts.

These are later judge samples selected conditionally on formatting/group completeness,
not independent replicates. Cached valid Opus calls are reused rather than resampled.
The original campaign's `completed_with_errors` status remains historical evidence even
when a separately recorded recovery provides the missing valid response. Accepted raw-call
hashes are associated with recovery logs explicitly. Internal CLI/schema repairs are not
fully enumerated as separate calls; retained driver/format failures are counted separately
from scientific disagreement or false criticism.

## Remaining scientific assessment

All 18 generation reports and all 48 repaired whole-report comparisons finished.
The original v3 condition has another 48 separately retained comparisons. Criticism-level
assessment includes 11 valid v2 outputs plus the explicitly excluded partial diagnostic
above. This completes a broad training diagnostic with a documented protocol failure,
not a fully successful native-criticism experiment. Targeted inspections above are
post-generation development checks, not expert adjudication or a random correctness sample.

Qualified assessors still need to review high-impact/disputed criticisms, whole rationales,
remedy burden and unique audit contributions with complete primary sources and inspectable
images where required. Build an expert-checked atomic inventory alongside a reproducible
sample, repeat generation to estimate variance, compare the added audit with equally costly
broad reading, and select an independent corpus after development. More ledger operations,
more published findings and preference wins cannot substitute for those checks.
