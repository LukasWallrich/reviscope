# Disposition: listwise ranking review

Adopted required six-criterion outputs, exact label coverage for overall and criterion ranks, deblinded stored criterion results, AI generator provenance, configurable manifest composition checks, and an explicit ceiling on implied pairwise counts. Shared seeded orders across judge families are intentional to make judge-family results directly comparable.

The ranker remains generic for pools of 2–12 reviews; the four-human/three-AI requirement is declared by the pilot manifest. It checkpoints each completed presentation and resumes only missing indices. Aggregation rejects incomplete presentations, duplicate judge identities, mismatched configs, and missing review ranks.
