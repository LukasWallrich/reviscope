# Disposition: pragmatic preprint-proxy protocol

Fable returned **SHIP with amendments**. The amendments were reviewed against the user's pragmatic-alpha goal; five were adopted and five were narrowed or rejected where they would waste model calls, create arbitrary thresholds, or confuse review-driven revision with version mismatch.

1. **Modified.** A version-difference map is frozen before generation; it is later applied symmetrically to human and AI points, preferably review-origin-blind. The suggested arbitrary 20% cap was rejected: minor points qualify the ranking, while loss of substantive comparability prevents ranking.
2. Audited-proxy admission requires positive concordance on a minimum field set; sparse evidence becomes `audit_inconclusive`.
3. **Modified.** The proxy must predate feedback for the selected round. Earlier transfer-chain reviews require their own matched version and are not silently pooled.
4. **Modified.** ±7 days is the default discovery/analysis window and ±30 days is a prespecified sensitivity. Older proxies with stronger linkage may enter a separately labelled wider-window stratum; date is not an identity cutoff.
5. Memorization probes and outcomes are stratified by identity tier; later-version memory is identified as a risk.
6. **Modified.** Mismatch decisions are locked before AI inspection, and known substantial mismatches are excluded before generation to avoid needless model calls; attrition remains reported.
7. Version-specific timestamps and exact day gaps are mandatory; ±7-day default and ±30-day sensitivity results are reported when available.
8. **Modified.** The audit compares reviewer-cited locations and original-round facts across proxy, published version and exchange. Differences explained by review-driven revision are expected; unexplained round-relevant differences downgrade the record.
9. Excluded-case counts and reasons remain reported. No ranking is produced when substantive comparability is lost.
10. `exact` now requires an explicitly tied deposit, byte identity, or complete normalized-main-text equality with only documented metadata/rendering differences; fuzzy similarity cannot establish exactness.

The final protocol also prioritizes submission-integrated In Review deposits while correctly treating them as stronger provenance rather than automatic exactness. The ±7-day window remains a discovery heuristic.

The protocol is prospective documentation. The current corpus loader and manifests retain their exact-version-only gate; proxy support requires a later explicit schema and implementation change.
