## Bohn et al. (2021), Nature Human Behaviour

**Article:** Manuel Bohn, Michael Henry Tessler, Megan Merrick, and Michael C. Frank, “How young children integrate information sources to infer the meaning of words,” *Nature Human Behaviour* 5, 1046–1054. Journal DOI: [10.1038/s41562-021-01145-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01145-1).

**Empirical design:** The article tests a Bayesian developmental model against behavioural word-learning data from children aged 2–5. It combines two earlier experiments with a newly collected, preregistered third experiment (`n = 220`) and compares the proposed rational-integration model with alternative models that omit or combine information sources differently. This is developmental and social-cognitive psychology rather than a conventional adult social-psychology experiment.

## Search note

A bounded Crossref title-and-author match over recent Nature Human Behaviour records produced this candidate. Exact-title matching alone is inadequate because titles can change between preprint and publication, and the mutable PsyArXiv landing file can be replaced after review. The corpus manifest should therefore store the journal DOI, journal received date, preprint record date, primary-file ID, immutable file revision number and timestamp, signed day gap, and peer-review-file URL separately.
