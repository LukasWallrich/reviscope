The rejected framing was "constructive review for research in progress". Fuel itself isn't necessarily rejected: reconsider neutrally, but generate fresh directions. Give 8 short, memorable, credible names, each with rationale, dual-use descriptor, and real downside. Avoid forced acronyms, grandiose truth guarantees, generic AI suffixes, and merely recycling rejected names. End with ranked top3 and explain tradeoffs. Do not claim trademark/domain availability or conduct tools/research. Be inventive without being precious.
